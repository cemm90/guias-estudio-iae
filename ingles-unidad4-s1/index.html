⏰ Simple Past — Regular Verbs

Use Simple Past to talk about finished actions in the past. Add -ed to regular verbs.

✅ Positive
Subject + verb+ed
• I washed the dishes yesterday.
• She cooked dinner last night.
• We cleaned the room two days ago.
❌ Negative
Subject + didn't + verb (NO -ed!)
• I didn't wash the dishes. (NOT: didn't washed)
• She didn't cook dinner.
Base formSimple PastExample sentence
cookcookedDad cooked soup last Saturday
washwashedI washed the dishes after dinner
cleancleanedShe cleaned the kitchen
vacuumvacuumedMy sister vacuumed the floor
waterwateredWe watered the plants last Friday
exerciseexercisedI exercised at the park yesterday
brushbrushedHe brushed his teeth this morning
⏰ Past time expressions: yesterday · last night · last Monday · last weekend · last Friday · two days ago · three days ago

🔍 There is / There are + Some / Any

THERE IS
For singular or uncountable nouns
• There is an apple. (1 apple)
• There is some water. (water = uncountable)
• There isn't any milk. ❌
THERE ARE
For plural nouns
• There are 3 apples. (plural)
• There are some tomatoes. ✅
• There aren't any mushrooms. ❌
✅ Positive → use SOME❌ Negative → use ANY
Singular / UncountableThere is some waterThere isn't any water
PluralThere are some tomatoesThere aren't any tomatoes
💡 Key rule: SOME = positive sentences  |  ANY = negative sentences

⏱️ How Often? — Frequency Expressions

Use "How often...?" to ask about how many times something happens.

1️⃣
once
= one time
2️⃣
twice
= two times
3️⃣
three times
(number + times)
🔢
four times
(number + times)
Pattern: once/twice/three times + a day / a week / a month / a year

• Q: How often do you brush your teeth?
  A: I brush my teeth twice a day. 🦷

• Q: How often do you exercise?
  A: I exercise three times a week. 🏃

• Q: How often do you go to the dentist?
  A: I go to the dentist twice a year. 🏥

📖 Reading Comprehension
📝 Activity 6: Read the text and answer the questions

Read carefully — some answers need inference! 🧠 Look for clues in the text.

My name is Sam. I live with my mom, dad, and little sister. We are a healthy family!

Every morning, I eat fruit and vegetables for breakfast — never cereal! I exercise three times a week at the park near my house. My sister vacuums the floor and I always wash the dishes after dinner.

Last Saturday, my dad cooked a big pot of soup. He added tomatoes, mushrooms, and a little oil. Mom set the table and I helped her. My sister cleaned the kitchen after. It was delicious!
